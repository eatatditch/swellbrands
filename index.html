The why

A holding company that surfs.

Swell Brands exists to build small, honest brands at the intersection of surf, hospitality, and craft — companies that feel like a place, not a product line.

We're independent. We move slowly. We'd rather make one thing well than ten things fast. Every brand we launch has to pass the same test: would we want to spend a Saturday inside it?

Note from the founders
"We started this because the lineup is the best room we've ever been in. Everything we make is trying to bring a little of that energy to land."
